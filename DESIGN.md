# WhatsFast design

WhatsFast uses a **green chat bubble** as its app icon. The product is **unofficial** and not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

Source artwork: [packaging/icons/whatsfast-1024.png](packaging/icons/whatsfast-1024.png).

The empty message composer is 35% taller than one text line, the first line sits in the vertical middle, and the bubble keeps 20% more space above the window's bottom edge than the old 8px inset. Reason: a tight one-line field against the window reads cheap; extra air does not change send keys.
